OUR STORY

Founded in 2005 in Tokyo, NXT Sports began as a small tuning shop with a passion for Japanese performance cars. What started as a humble garage has grown into one of the most respected names in the automotive aftermarket industry.

Our philosophy is simple: blend cutting-edge technology with traditional Japanese craftsmanship. Each product we develop undergoes rigorous testing on both street and track to ensure it meets our exacting standards.

In 2012, we expanded our operations to include full vehicle customization services. Our team of engineers and designers work closely with each client to create truly unique builds that reflect their personality and driving style.

Today, NXT Sports products are used by professional racing teams worldwide, and our custom builds have been featured in numerous automotive publications. Despite our growth, we remain committed to the hands-on approach that made us successful.
